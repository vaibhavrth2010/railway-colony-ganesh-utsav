Our Ganesh Utsav

🪔

Railway Colony Ganesh Utsav Samiti

Railway Colony Ganesh Utsav Samiti celebrates the divine presence of Lord Ganesha with devotion, unity, cultural activities and beautiful memories shared by the community.

From pandal preparation and Ganesh Ji Aagman to puja, aarti, events and visarjan, every moment is celebrated together with faith and enthusiasm.
